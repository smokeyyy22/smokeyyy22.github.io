Dossier

I'm a 4th-year cybersecurity engineering student, equally interested in how systems get broken and how they get defended. I split my time between red-team style projects — building attack infrastructure, simulating intrusions — and the blue-team side: threat hunting, SIEM tuning, and making sense of noisy logs.

Lately I've been combining that with AI — using LLMs and RAG to speed up threat analysis instead of doing it all by hand. I like projects that force me to understand a system end to end, not just use a tool someone else built.

Outside of coursework, I spend a lot of time in CTFs and on HackTheBox, chipping away at boxes for fun as much as practice.
