About me

I'm Ahnaf Akif — a filmmaker, visual storyteller, and educator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

What started as a curiosity for the camera grew into a career spanning photography, videography, and social media content creation, accumulating quite a lot of views across projects. That foundation sharpened my eye for visual storytelling — and today, I channel it into short narrative films, music videos, and commercial productions where craft and intention drive every frame.
